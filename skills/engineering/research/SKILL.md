# Research

- State the decision and what evidence would change it.
- Search primary sources before summaries.
- Record source URL, version or date, observed claim, confidence, and open question.
- Reproduce API or runtime behavior locally when possible.
- Separate facts from recommendations.
- End with a decision, a blocked question, or a reason not to proceed.

Do not turn research into a literature review. Stop when the next product decision is safe enough to make.
