# To Questionnaire

- State the decision and the deadline or dependency.
- Separate known facts from questions.
- Number only the blocking questions.
- Offer a recommended answer and the alternative consequence.
- Include examples or acceptance checks where a word could be interpreted two ways.
- End with the artifact or command that can resume work after answers arrive.

Do not use this to avoid making a reversible local choice.
